For shops and brands

How your products would appear

We put products in front of people whose taste they suit, and send them to your site to buy.

  • Each product shows its brand, your shop name, the current price and a direct link to your site.
  • Products appear in personal picks, outfit and room ideas, and gift lists.
  • People buy on your site. We don't handle orders.
  • No voucher codes or cashback.
  • We don't bid on your brand name in search ads.
  • Style Signature is in private testing, so its audience is small for now.
